# Print plan

Status: v1 display build, 2026-10-04; updated for the review fix round 2026-10-05. Printer: Bambu
Lab A1 / P1S / X1 (256 mm cube), 0.4 mm nozzle, one material per plate (no AMS). Files:
`out/cad/stl/<part>.stl`, already in print orientation (on z = 0, mm). The numbers here are
generated by `hydroegg.cad.assembly.print_plan()` into `out/cad/checks/assembly.json`
(`print_plan`) and reprinted by `scripts/build.py`.

Material choice for the display build: PLA for the shells, rotor, fin blanks and generator replica
(stiff, prints the 45 deg overhangs and bridges cleanly); PETG for the parts that see torque,
sliding or a press fit with a set screw (claws, carrier, spacer) and for the fins (tough root in the
tow case). The v2 wet build moves the shells, hub, spinner and cap to PETG too.

## 1. Print the tolerance coupons first

`tolerance_coupon.stl` with its two keys, and `tolerance_coupon_b.stl`, in each material you will
use and with the same slicer profile as the real parts (one plate, plate 0).

* Coupon A holds the 608 / 688 seats, pin holes, an insert hole, the M12 tap drill, a bayonet key
  and a fin dovetail key.
* Coupon B holds the hub D-bore (D8.10 / 8.15 / 8.25 with the 0.5 mm flat: try it on a shaft
  offcut), the block magnet seats 6.0 x 2.0 / 6.1 x 2.1 / 6.2 x 2.2 and the disc seats D4.0 / 4.1 /
  4.2, each printed vertical (as in the hub and the tail bosses) and horizontal in the -Y face (as
  in the blade heel and shank), and an upright 3.7 mm ear with horizontal D2.9 / 3.0 / 3.1 holes
  (as the hub ears; a 3 mm pin should press into the D2.9).

Pick the seat, pin and insert diameters that give the right feel and adjust `PRESS_FIT` / hole
compensation before printing the rest (the shells use crush ribs: translate the coupon result to
the rib tips, see `coupon.json` how_to_use).

Every STL is watertight with consistent winding and fits the bed. Totals for one display build
(coupons and cutaway shells excluded): **PLA about 207 g, PETG about 38 g, 244 g in all**, plus
supports, brims and the coupons (about 33 g per material).

Notes per part (the full text is in each part's `notes` in `assembly.json`):

* **front_shell**: nose up on its equator face, no supports. The 38.6 mm nose-plug flat bridge is the
  only bridge (cooling 100 %, bridge speed about 25 mm/s; expect a little sag on that internal
  face). The tow eye is a teardrop lug pointing up with a 2 mm flat on its hole apex. The fin pads
  (R2 root blend, 0.5 mm chamfer on the groove mouth) stand on the bed.
* **aft_shell**: tail end face down, spigot up, no supports. Pocket rims carry a 0.6 x 45 deg
  chamfer; the pocket lips undercut in-slice at 45 deg. The 0.6 mm seal stop ring is the only
  sub-rule face.
* **hub**: front face down, no supports; slot-end bridges up to 14.6 mm; the ear holes are
  horizontal and come out slightly oval (ream if the pins will not start; coupon B tells you).
  At least 4 perimeters: the heel slot floor is 1.23 mm over the shaft bore.
* **spinner**: base down, tip up, no supports, 5 mm brim. Each window's full-depth part closes with
  45 deg flanks and a flat bridge of at most 15 mm anchored on both flanks (3 bridges at print
  z 17.3); the D22.6 seat ceiling is a flat bridge on a complete ring of the inner cone. Break the
  window edges by hand (the 0.4 mm chamfer is not modelled).
* **spinner_cap**: spigot down, 3 mm brim, 100 % infill, no supports (45 deg shoulder). Small part:
  print it with the rotor plate or with minimum layer time on.
* **blade** (x 3): chord vertical, trailing edge down, knuckle end face on the bed, pin bore
  vertical. Tree supports, build plate only, under the trailing edge (it floats 1.7 to 3.7 mm
  above the bed; 1.7 to 2.2 along the aero span), the heel underside and the shank TE ramp (126 mm2
  in all). Support settings: top Z distance 0.2 mm, dense support interface (2 to 3 layers,
  0.2 mm spacing), 5 mm brim on the knuckle.
* **claw_b**: aft face down, dogs up. The collar-pocket bridge prints as a full D16.2 disc over a
  0.2 mm sacrificial membrane across the bore: drill the membrane out with an 8.4 mm drill.
* **claw_a**: flange down, dogs up; the dogs reach the flange OD (1.2 mm skin over the counterbores).
* **motor_carrier**: AFT face down (tabs flush with it), counterbores up, no supports.
* **generator_stator**: front face down; the 4 carrier screws self-tap into its D2.5 pilots (45 deg
  lead-in), no inserts.
* **fin** (x 2): root down, span up (leans 15 deg in its own plane), no supports. Brim ears at the
  two root ends only (a full brim would bond to the tongue's sliding faces); slow outer walls.
* **fin_blank** (x 2): bottom down, top face up, 100 % infill, no supports.
* **tolerance_coupon_b**: flat as modelled, no supports; its horizontal test holes overhang on
  purpose (63.5 mm2).

Why these groups: plate 0 is the coupons, printed before anything else; plate 1 holds the
support-free PLA parts with the same 6-wall profile family (shells, generator replica, fin
blanks); plate 2 isolates the only parts that need supports (the blades) together with the other
rotor parts, so a failed support does not cost the shells; plate 3 is the one PETG job; the
cutaway shells are an alternative to the plate 1 shells for the display.

## 4. After printing

* Shells: tap M12x1.5 in the nose seat (10.5 mm drill modelled); press the bearings with a draw
  bolt (crush ribs set the fit); glue the three D4 x 2 tail magnets into the detent bosses (face
  0.05 mm below the boss face, attracting the blades' shank magnets).
* Hub: tap the grub bore M3; ream the D2.90 ears if the pins will not start; glue the three
  6 x 2 x 2 block magnets into the stop-face seats (CA, face 0.2 mm below the stop face). The hinge
  pins are pressed only after each blade is dropped into its slot (assembly step 10).
* Blades: remove the tree supports; glue one 6 x 2 x 2 block in the heel seat (0.2 mm below the
  stop face) and one D4 x 2 disc in the shank seat (0.1 mm below its mouth), polarity attracting
  the hub and tail magnets. Ream the disc seat to 4.0 if it sags.
* Spinner and cap: check the cap's crush ribs in the D6 bore (a firm push, the fingernail groove
  pulls it); sand the window edges.
* Generator replica: melt the 2 M3 inserts into the bell; the stator takes the screws directly.
* Claw B: drill the 0.2 mm membrane out (8.4 mm); press the Ruland collar in (3 crush ribs), a drop
  of CA locks it.
* Fins and blanks: slide on, pin with ISO 8734 2 x 10 (a 1.75 mm filament stub works for display).
