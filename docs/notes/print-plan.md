# Print plan

Status: v1 display build, 2026-10-04; updated for the review fix round, the polish round and the
review 2 round of 2026-10-05 (bayonet-click breakaway ties, one detent boss top, fin root flare and
tip lands, strip nail groove and trimmed tip, finer blade cradle, standing tongue key and tilted
disc seats, own plate for the generator replica) and the nose round of 2026-10-05 (clean display nose:
the display build prints `front_shell` and has no blanking plug, a v2 build prints `front_shell_v2`;
softer slot and strip outline), refreshed from the fresh integration build of 2026-10-05. Printer: Bambu Lab A1 / P1S / X1 (256 mm cube), 0.4 mm nozzle, one material per plate
(no AMS). Files: `out/cad/stl/<part>.stl`, already in print orientation (on z = 0, mm). The numbers
here are generated by `ovo.cad.assembly.print_plan()` into `out/cad/checks/assembly.json`
(`print_plan`) and reprinted by `scripts/build.py`.

Material choice for the display build: PLA for the shells, rotor, blanking strips and generator
replica (stiff, prints the 45 deg overhangs and bridges cleanly); PETG for the parts that
see torque, sliding or a press fit with a set screw (claws, carrier, spacer) and for the fins (tough
root in the tow case). The v2 wet build moves the shells, hub, spinner and cap to PETG too.

Colours (owner decision 2026-10-05): one ivory for everything on the outside of the egg (shells,
hub, spinner, spinner cap, fins, blanking strips), orange for the three blades, blue
for the generator replica (display only). The drivetrain internals (claws, carrier, spacer) can be
any colour; the renders show them charcoal. The blade cradles are sacrificial and print in the same
job and filament as their blade.

## 1. Print the tolerance coupons first

`tolerance_coupon.stl` with its two window keys, the standing tongue key and `tolerance_coupon_b.stl`, in
each material you will
use and with the same slicer profile as the real parts (one plate, plate 0).

* Coupon A holds the 608 / 688 seats, pin holes, an insert hole, the M12 tap drill, a bayonet key
  and a fin dovetail key printed floor face down (as the blanking strip); the standing key
  `tolerance_coupon_key_tongue_up` (tongue up on a 10 mm stub) prints as the fin does (review P7).
* Coupon B holds the hub D-bore (D8.10 / 8.15 / 8.25 with the 0.5 mm flat: try it on a shaft
  offcut), the block magnet seats 6.0 x 2.0 / 6.1 x 2.1 / 6.2 x 2.2 and the disc seats D4.0 / 4.1 /
  4.2, each printed vertical (as in the hub) and horizontal in the -Y face (as in the blade heel and
  shank), a tilted row of the disc seats on the sloped +X end (axes 49 deg from vertical, as the
  aft-shell detent bosses; D4.0 / 4.1 / 4.2 from the -Y edge), and an upright 3.7 mm ear with
  horizontal D2.9 / 3.0 / 3.1 holes (as the hub ears; a 3 mm pin should press into the D2.9).

Pick the seat, pin and insert diameters that give the right feel and adjust `PRESS_FIT` / hole
compensation before printing the rest (the shells use crush ribs: translate the coupon result to
the rib tips, see `coupon.json` how_to_use).

Every STL is watertight with consistent winding, has 0 folded sliver pairs and fits the bed.
Totals for one display build, with the coupons and the cutaway shells excluded and the three blade
cradles included: **PLA about 204 g, PETG about 31 g, 235 g in all**, plus brims and the coupons
(about 34 g per material). The display build prints `front_shell`; `front_shell_v2` (58 g PETG,
plate 6) and the cutaway shells are alternatives, outside these totals.

Notes per part (the full text is in each part's `notes` in `assembly.json`):

* **front_shell**: nose up on its equator face, no supports. The 38.6 mm nose-plug flat bridge is the
  only bridge (cooling 100 %, bridge speed about 25 mm/s; expect a little sag on that internal
  face). The tow eye is a teardrop lug pointing up with a 2 mm flat on its hole apex. The fin rails
  are recessed into bosses on the inner wall (no pads); each boss ends on a 45 deg cone, and the
  bayonet channel cutters' front faces below the lip lean 45 deg, so no flat ledge faces the bed. The slot
  mouths have R2 aft corners and a straight taper on one side (their walls stand vertical in print). The
  pocket tips narrow at 45 deg where they follow the blade tips (3 x 2 mm2 of pocket wall reach 54 to 57
  deg there: harmless). This is the DISPLAY shell: a clean egg nose with the tow eye only, no gland seat
  and no bore (owner decision 2026-10-05).
* **front_shell_v2** (v2 build only, replaces front_shell; PETG for the wet build): the same shell with the
  raised M12 gland seat, blended into the nose through an R6 fillet (concave, between two printable
  faces), and the 10.5 mm tap-drill bore. Same orientation and settings. Tap the bore M12x1.5 and screw
  the cable gland in.
* **aft_shell**: tail end face down, spigot up, no supports. Pocket rims carry a 0.6 x 45 deg
  chamfer; the pocket lips undercut in-slice at 45 deg. The stowed-detent bosses lean 49 deg to
  face the blade seat (owner call T15) and start on a 2.5 mm wide chin swept down into the
  root-notch ramp. Boss and chin share one top that never leans beyond the 49 deg boss plane and
  never steps down toward the bed (review C2 / P2), so the layer scan of the STL (slicer mid-planes
  0.1 + 0.2 k, and the 0.05 and 0.15 phases) finds no island. The bayonet click tines in the spigot
  wall have vertical slits and 45 deg underside slots (0.81 mm normal). **Breakaway ties**: the STL
  carries a 0.8 mm tie across each tine's free-end slit at the tine's bottom, so the underside's
  first lines print as a bridge anchored at both ends (review C1 / P1). After printing, push a
  hobby knife straight down each of the 3 slits (beside the bump) to cut its tie, then press each
  tine in with a thumb: it must spring back freely. The 0.6 mm seal stop ring is the only other
  sub-rule face.
* **hub**: front face down, no supports; slot-end bridges up to 14.6 mm; the ear holes are
  horizontal and come out slightly oval (ream if the pins will not start; coupon B tells you).
  At least 4 perimeters: the heel slot floor is 1.23 mm over the shaft bore.
* **spinner**: base down, tip up, no supports, 5 mm brim. The three windows (review V1) are tight
  and cut normal to the skin; each closes at the top in a rounded arch and a flat bridge of about
  12 mm (expect a slight droop at the very top of each window). The D22.6 seat ceiling is a flat
  bridge on a complete ring of the inner cone, pierced by the D3.4 screw hole and the three D3.4 peg
  holes, so the lines beside the holes are short cantilevers (up to ~8 mm): it is the hub clamp face
  and the axial datum (T12), so face it flat on sandpaper over glass before assembly.
* **spinner_cap**: spigot down, 3 mm brim, 100 % infill, no supports (45 deg shoulder). Small part:
  print it with the rotor plate or with minimum layer time on.
* **blade** (x 3): chord vertical, trailing edge down, knuckle end face on the bed, pin bore
  vertical. The trailing edge floats 1.7 to 3.7 mm above the bed and the heel underside 2 mm.
  **Use the cradle, not slicer supports**: load `blade.stl` and `blade_cradle.stl` together as one
  object. They share the same print coordinates; Bambu Studio asks whether to load the two files
  as one object with multiple parts: yes. Turn supports off.
  * The cradle is two low rails, up to 2.8 mm tall: one under the heel, one under the shank ramp,
    the trailing edge and the tip.
  * Each rail's top is 0.2 mm under the blade (the support top-Z distance), measured every 0.25 mm
    along the span (review P6), and the rail is 1.6 mm wide or as wide as the blade's lowest band.
  * It carries 71 % of the 126 mm2 of faces beyond 45 deg (it was 57 %): the top sits 0.20 to 0.33 mm
    under the blade along the whole span (`te_gap.py` probe, every 0.5 mm). The rest are the bridged
    ceilings of the heel slot and the shank seat (inside the blade) and faces within one layer of the bed
    next to the knuckle.
  * Why the cradle: it holds the long thin trailing edge on a straight, continuous top at a known
    gap, it never touches the aerofoil faces, and it snaps off in one piece.
  * Fallback: tree supports, build plate only, top Z distance 0.2 mm, dense interface (2 to 3
    layers).
  * 5 mm brim on the knuckle.
* **claw_b**: aft face down, dogs up. The collar-pocket bridge prints as a full D16.2 disc over a
  0.2 mm sacrificial membrane across the bore: drill the membrane out with an 8.4 mm drill.
* **claw_a**: flange down, dogs up; the dogs reach the flange OD (1.2 mm skin over the counterbores).
* **motor_carrier**: AFT face down (tabs flush with it), counterbores up, no supports. The four D6.0
  counterbores leave a 1.0 mm web to the D16 centre bore, which has no chamfer on that face (review P9;
  the web is not structural, T23).
* **generator_stator**: front face down; the 4 carrier screws self-tap into its D2.5 pilots (45 deg
  lead-in), no inserts. The real motor's M2.5 holes on PCD 40 are D1.6 x 0.6 dimples on PCD 39 on
  the replica (review P9: the holes left a 0.24 mm first-layer rim). Blue PLA on its own plate (5).
* **fin** (x 2): tip down (span vertical), tongue on top, no supports: the tongue's undercut flank
  and the root flare beside the neck are 45 deg (43 in section), and the tip corners end on a short
  land at their 42 deg point (review C4 / P3). It is a slender tower (73.7 mm on a 3.6 mm wide first
  layer, centre of mass 1.6 mm inside the footprint edge): a 5 mm brim all round, outer walls at
  50 mm/s or less, a 6 to 8 s minimum layer time, and on an A1 (bed moving in Y) set the chord along
  Y (plate 3 is packed that way, review P4). The tongue's aft corners are rounded like the slot's, and
  its root flare rises from the tongue's own edges (wider on the tapered side near the leading edge).
  Glue the 4 x 2 x 2 magnet into the tongue seat (details in `fins.json`).
* **fin_blank** (x 2): floor face down (the magnet seat opens on the bed), 100 % infill, no
  supports. Its flush outer face slopes about 29 deg, so print the strips at 0.08 to 0.12 mm layers
  (or variable layer height) with ironing off; the entry wedge is trimmed at 0.6 mm with a 0.3 mm
  break (review P5). The outline is a tapered inlay with rounded ends, and a half-round fingernail
  notch (R1.5, 0.8 deep) sits at its aft end; it replaces the C6 groove across the face.
* **tolerance_coupon_key_tongue_up**: stands on its 10 mm stub, tongue up (as the fin), 3 mm brim.
* **tolerance_coupon_b**: flat as modelled, no supports; its horizontal test holes overhang on
  purpose, and its sloped +X end carries the D4.0 / 4.1 / 4.2 disc seats with their axes 49 deg from
  vertical, as the aft shell's detent bosses print (review P7).

Why these groups:

* Plate 0 is the coupons, printed before anything else.
* Plate 1 holds the support-free ivory PLA parts with the same 6-wall profile family: shells and
  blanking strips. One material and one colour (review P10: the blue replica moved off it). There is
  no blanking plug any more: the display nose is clean.
* Plate 2 is the rotor: orange blades on their cradles, with the ivory hub, spinner and cap. With
  one colour per plate, print the blades and the ivory rotor parts as two jobs (or one with a
  filament change).
* Plate 3 is the one PETG job; the fins stand with their chord along Y (review P4: an A1's bed
  moves in Y, so its accelerations act along the fin's stiff direction).
* Plate 4: the cutaway shells are an alternative to the plate 1 shells for the display.
* Plate 5 is the blue generator replica (bell and stator), its own colour and job.
* Plate 6 is the v2 front shell. It replaces the plate 1 front shell in a v2 (wet) build only; it is
  not part of the display build totals.

## 4. After printing

* Shells: cut the three bayonet-click ties first (a hobby knife straight down each free-end slit;
  each tine must then spring back when pressed). The display front shell has nothing to do at the
  nose; the v2 front shell's 10.5 mm seat bore is tapped M12x1.5 for the gland. Press the bearings
  with a draw bolt (crush ribs set the fit); glue the three D4 x 2 tail magnets into the detent bosses (face
  0.05 mm below the boss face, attracting the blades' shank magnets).
* Hub: tap the grub bore M3; ream the D2.90 ears if the pins will not start; glue the three
  6 x 2 x 2 block magnets into the stop-face seats (CA, face 0.2 mm below the stop face). The hinge
  pins are pressed only after each blade is dropped into its slot (assembly step 10).
* Blades: snap the cradle off (or remove the tree supports) and sand the trailing edge lightly;
  glue one 6 x 2 x 2 block in the heel seat (0.2 mm below the stop face) and one D4 x 2 disc in the
  shank seat (0.1 mm below its mouth), polarity attracting the hub and tail magnets. Ream the disc
  seat to 4.0 if it sags.
* Spinner and cap: face the D22.6 clamp ring flat on sandpaper over glass (it is the axial datum);
  check the cap's crush ribs in the D6 bore (a firm push, the fingernail groove pulls it). The
  spinner goes on over the hub with the blades stowed (its tight windows pass the stowed roots).
* Generator replica: melt the 2 M3 inserts into the bell; the stator takes the screws directly.
* Claw B: drill the 0.2 mm membrane out (8.4 mm); press the Ruland collar in (3 crush ribs), a drop
  of CA locks it.
* Fins and blanks: glue the N52 4 x 2 x 2 blocks into the tongue, strip and groove-floor seats
  (polarity attracting); slide the fin aft and in to the stop, or the strip for packing. To take a
  strip out, hook a fingernail in the notch at its aft end and drag it forward.
